Title: Multimorbidity care pathways (demonstration)
Source: DEA-MedRAG synthetic corpus / clinical_guidelines
Year: 2025

For adults with hypertension and hyperlipidemia, the demonstration pathway prioritizes
continued blood-pressure control, medication reconciliation, and periodic monitoring.
Treatment changes require clinician review of symptoms, laboratory trends, allergies,
patient goals, and the complete medication list.

For adults with type 2 diabetes and chronic kidney disease, the demonstration pathway
describes an SGLT2-inhibitor cardio-renal pathway as a possible topic for clinician
review. The text does not authorize prescribing and does not replace renal dosing
assessment.

For atrial fibrillation with chronic kidney disease, anticoagulation decisions should
be individualized using renal function, bleeding history, fall history, concurrent
medications, and patient preferences. A recent fall or dizziness should trigger a
careful safety review before any treatment change.
